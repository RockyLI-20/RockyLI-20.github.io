How to read the diagnostics

A symmetric Normal proposal gives acceptance probability min(1, target(proposal) / target(current)). A rejected step repeats the current state. Small steps can give high acceptance and highly correlated draws. Large steps can trigger frequent rejections.

ACF measures dependence at each lag. ESS here is a per-chain estimate using the initial positive pairs of autocorrelations through lag 40, on up to 1,000 states. It appears after 20 steps. Read it alongside the traces and whether each chain explores both sides of a two-peak target. Long traces keep the first and last states and show the highest and lowest values in each group of nearby iterations. The live density estimates use each chain’s latest 200 draws with a fixed Gaussian kernel bandwidth of 0.3.

Changing proposal SD during a run layers together results from two proposal widths. Reset chains to compare settings from the same starting states. STAT 6206 Chapter 2, pp. 7–11. Stan reference on effective sample size.
