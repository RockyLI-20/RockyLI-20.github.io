Statistical Appendix Writing Skill

Introduction: An AI skill for drafting, revising, and reviewing statistical appendices, with a focus on proof structure, notation, assumptions, and citation checks. Designed around the paper’s own statistical claims, it supports a clear route from theorem targets to a readable proof.

Explore the workflow

Identify the statistical problem, intended claims, and the theoretical results that the paper needs.

Work backwards from each result to the assumptions and lemmas that support it.

Align notation across the manuscript and make the role of each assumption explicit.

Develop or examine proofs while preserving the model, estimator, and scope of the paper.

Check sources, references, and the readability of the mathematical argument.
